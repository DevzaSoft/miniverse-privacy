See Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin data disclosure.

2. Purposes

  • Store local game progress/preferences and operate the game.
  • Validate rewarded-ad transactions and prevent duplicate rewards.
  • Present and manage required privacy choices.
  • Respond to support and data-protection requests initiated by users.
  • Support advertising, measurement/analytics, diagnostics and fraud-prevention functions described by Google's advertising SDK.

Local game files are stored on the device; no single day-based automatic deletion period has been identified for all local records. Some reward receipts and recovery copies may remain. Records held in Google and other provider systems are subject to those providers' own retention policies. Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards are used; absolute security is not guaranteed.
