Data Access and Deletion Policy

The app is intended for a general family audience that includes children, teens and adults. It does not verify age or collect dates of birth. The app must therefore be configured on the assumption that children may access it, including its advertising and third-party SDK data practices.

Version: 1.0 · Date: 8 November 2026

1. What data is present in the app?

The game has no user account. Level/city progress, stars, fuel, achievements and game preferences are stored in app files on the device. Reward receipts may also be stored locally to prevent duplicate rewarded-ad benefits. The app has no in-app email, phone or contact form. If a user voluntarily emails support, that correspondence may be stored by the email service. Google advertising/UMP services may process technical and advertising-related data in their own systems.

2. Actions available on the user's device

  • Reset Settings: restores sound, brightness, tutorial and similar preferences to defaults; it does not erase all level progress, fuel or reward receipts.
  • Clear Android app data: may cause local progress and preferences to be lost. This may not be reversible.
  • Uninstall the app: may remove some app-owned local files; it does not automatically delete operating-system/account backups or third-party provider records.
  • UMP privacy options: manage advertising/privacy choices; they do not erase all data held by Google or other providers.

3. Retention and deletion limits

No general timed automatic deletion mechanism has been identified for local game data. Reward receipts and some temporary/recovery copies may remain. Support and privacy-request emails should be kept only while necessary to respond to the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Deletion of Google/UMP, email and operating-system records depends on the relevant providers' tools and policies. The developer cannot guarantee remote deletion of device or provider copies that are not accessible to the developer.

4. Access, correction and deletion requests

For privacy requests, email devzasoft@gmail.com with the subject “MiniVerse — privacy request”. State which right you wish to exercise and the relevant data category. A valid request must satisfy the method and information required by applicable law; any identity verification will be proportionate to the request. We will assess the scope and act only on records accessible to us or properly requestable from the relevant provider. If local game files cannot be accessed remotely, we will explain the options available on the device.

5. Response and contact

Requests under Turkish KVKK will be answered as soon as possible and no later than 30 days, subject to statutory requirements. Where provider-held records are involved, the request may need to be referred to the provider or handled through its own tools. Any action that cannot be completed and any inaccessible copies will be explained. Contact: devzasoft@gmail.com.
