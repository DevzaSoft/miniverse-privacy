MiniVerse Road Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Publisher: DEVZASOFT
Contact: devzasoft@gmail.com
Last updated: 9 November 2026

MiniVerse Road Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game provided by DEVZASOFT.

Game data

Level progress, stars, preferences and other game saves are stored on your device. DEVZASOFT does not collect these records on its own server and does not require you to create a player account. Operating-system backup services may operate separately.

Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob advertising. Google and its advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, security and related purposes.

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy. Where required, advertising privacy choices are provided through Google's consent management system.

Third-party services

The app may interact with Google Play and Google AdMob. Their data processing practices are governed by their own policies.

Children's privacy

MiniVerse Road Puzzle is designed for users aged 16 and older. The app does not ask for a date of birth or age.

Data deletion and contact

You can clear locally stored game data through your device's app settings. This may erase your progress. Operating-system backups may need to be managed separately.

For privacy questions, contact devzasoft@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as needed. The current version will be published on this page.
