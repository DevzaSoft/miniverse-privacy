MiniVerse: Road Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Document version: 1.0 · Effective date: 2026-11-09

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the MiniVerse: Road Puzzle mobile app and services DEVZASOFT provides in connection with it. It describes the app's known data practices and does not replace the privacy notices of third-party providers.

2. Accounts and game data stored on your device

The app does not provide user account registration, Google sign-in, email/password membership or an in-app profile. Game progress, stars, best moves, city/level progress, fuel and renewal information, and sound, brightness, language and tutorial preferences are stored in app files on the device. Local reward/transaction receipts may also be stored to prevent duplicate rewarded-ad benefits. No feature that automatically uploads ordinary game saves to a developer-operated server has been identified. Android device or account backups may create separate copies; exclusive on-device storage is not guaranteed.

3. Age and intended audience

The publisher's intended audience is ages 16–17 and ages 18 and over. The app is not declared as designed for children; users under 16 are not an intended target group. This does not establish a particular user's verified age or adulthood. The app asks for neither age nor date of birth and contains no age list, age classification or age-correction screen. First launch contains only language selection, a privacy notice and a separate Terms acceptance flow. Users aged 16–17 are not treated as adults; local contractual capacity, parent/legal representative and data-processing requirements need separate assessment. The audience decision alone is not worldwide legal compliance or Google Play approval.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

The intended release of MiniVerse Road Puzzle includes optional rewarded advertising through Google AdMob. Google UMP manages applicable privacy choices and required consent flows. Advertising SDK initialization and ad requests require the applicable privacy processing and current SDK eligibility checks to complete first. Terms acceptance, viewing a privacy notice and advertising consent are separate. Declining advertising consent or an unavailable advertising service does not prevent ordinary offline gameplay. Privacy Options are accessible again from Settings when required by the SDK.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be used to present and manage privacy choices where required. UMP choices are separate from acceptance of the Terms and from data-erasure requests. Consent to personalised advertising is not presented as a mandatory condition for playing. Privacy options are made available again where required by the SDK and applicable rules.

5. Automatically collected technical data and advertising SDK

The project includes Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin and Google UMP components. Google's disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin states that, depending on configuration and use, the SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The absence of the AD_ID permission in the inspected Android manifest does not prove that App Set ID or every other possible identifier is never processed. Actual data flows may vary by SDK version, enabled features, device and region. See Google's Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

This does not mean that the app requests GPS/location permission. The app does not ask users in-app for a username, email address, phone number, date of birth, photo or contact form; it does not collect dates of birth for age verification. No age classification is created or read by the current product flow. This does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no technical data of its own. If a user independently emails support, the sender address, message and attachments may be processed through email services.

8. Retention and deletion

No general timed automatic deletion mechanism has been identified for local game data. Reward receipts and temporary/recovery copies may remain. Reset Settings does not erase all progress, fuel or reward records. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling may lose progress; backups and provider-held records may remain separately. Under the publisher’s decision, support emails are retained for 12 months after the relevant support case is closed. This does not establish a separate retention period for records of data-subject requests. Automatic deletion has not been verified; recording the closure date, tracking the period and carrying out deletion at its end are the publisher’s responsibility. Deletion of Google service records and backups inaccessible to the developer within the same period is not guaranteed. Google and other providers retain their own records under their policies; remote deletion of records inaccessible to the developer is not guaranteed.
