MiniVerse: Road Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Document version: 1.0 · Effective date: 8 November 2026

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the MiniVerse: Road Puzzle mobile app and services DEVZASOFT provides in connection with it. It describes the app's known data practices and does not replace the privacy notices of third-party providers.

2. Accounts and game data stored on your device

The app does not provide user account registration, Google sign-in, email/password membership or an in-app profile. Game progress, stars, best moves, city/level progress, fuel and renewal information, and sound, brightness, language and tutorial preferences are stored in app files on the device. Local reward/transaction receipts may also be stored to prevent duplicate rewarded-ad benefits. No feature that automatically uploads ordinary game saves to a developer-operated server has been identified. Android device or account backups may create separate copies; exclusive on-device storage is not guaranteed.

3. Age and intended audience

The app intentionally targets children aged 6–12, teens aged 13–17 and adults aged 18 and over with equal priority. For Google Play target-age declarations, the intended groups are 6–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17 and 18 and over; children aged 5 and under are not part of the target audience. This is not an adult-focused app that children may only happen to access. It currently does not ask for age, verify age or technically block access by minors. The audience statement is therefore not age verification or an access restriction for any particular age group. In Google Play Console, select only the age groups for which the game is genuinely designed and appropriate. UMP privacy choices are not age verification, a neutral age screen or parental permission. Google Play Families rules may impose specific ad-SDK and age-screening requirements for mixed-audience apps serving ads to children and users of unknown age.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

The app includes optional rewarded-ad placements for in-game benefits such as fuel, extra moves or hints. Users can choose not to watch ads and still play the core game; watching an ad is not a condition for access to all levels. Ad or reward availability is not guaranteed. A reward is granted only when the ad provider sends a valid completion callback and the game's applicable conditions are met. Production ad serving is disabled in the reviewed configuration; Google demo ad units are for development/testing only. This statement does not mean live ads have been activated. Because the app explicitly targets children aged 6–12 as well as older audiences, ads to children or users of unknown age must remain disabled for now. Before any future ad activation, verify Google Play Families requirements, an appropriate age-screening/segmentation path, child-directed ad treatment and a G maximum ad content rating on every relevant ad request, and the Families Self-Certified Ads SDK eligibility of every ad SDK and mediation adapter version.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be used to present and manage privacy choices where required. UMP choices are separate from acceptance of the Terms and from data-erasure requests. Consent to personalised advertising is not presented as a mandatory condition for playing. Privacy options are made available again where required by the SDK and applicable rules.

5. Automatically collected technical data and advertising SDK

The project includes Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin and Google UMP components. Google's disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin states that, depending on configuration and use, the SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The absence of the AD_ID permission in the inspected Android manifest does not prove that App Set ID or every other possible identifier is never processed. Actual data flows may vary by SDK version, enabled features, device and region. See Google's Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin data disclosure.

This does not mean that the app requests GPS/location permission. The app does not ask users in-app for a username, email address, phone number, date of birth, photo or contact form; it does not collect dates of birth for age verification. A legacy age-category record from an earlier version may be read for safety purposes; this record is not age verification or parental permission. This does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no technical data of its own. If a user independently emails support, the sender address, message and attachments may be processed through email services.

Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and UMP may be involved in advertising and privacy choices; Google Play distributes the app; an email provider may process support correspondence initiated by the user; and web hosting/network providers may be involved when this Policy page is visited. These providers may process data under their own services. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of data used by sites and apps that use Google services.

Personal data may be transferred to systems outside Türkiye through these services. However, the actual contracting entity for each feature, receiving legal entity, transfer destination country and applicable KVKK Article 9 mechanism have not been conclusively verified from the current project records. We therefore do not claim that data is transferred to a particular country, that a standard contract has been executed, or that every legal transfer condition has been completed. These details must be confirmed against the relevant provider agreements and enabled service configuration before publication.

7. Purposes of use

  • Store local game progress and apply user preferences.
  • Validate rewarded-ad completions and prevent duplicate rewards for the same transaction.
  • Present and manage privacy choices through UMP.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests that a user voluntarily sends.
  • Support the advertising, measurement/analytics, diagnostics and fraud-prevention functions described by the Google advertising SDK.

The applicable legal ground for each purpose under KVKK must be assessed in light of the specific data flow and provider roles. This Policy does not itself constitute consent; a separate valid legal ground or permission is required where applicable.

8. Retention and deletion

No general fixed day/month automatic deletion period has been identified for local game files. Some temporary or recovery copies and reward receipts may remain; no timed automatic purge has been identified for reward receipts. Reset Settings does not erase all game progress or all personal data. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling the app may cause local progress to be lost; operating-system/account backups and third-party provider records may remain separately. Support and privacy-request emails should be kept only as long as necessary to respond to the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Google and other providers retain their records under their own policies; the developer does not promise an unverified fixed period for those records.

Access to email contents should be restricted to the authorised developer account. Other appropriate technical and organisational measures are used in light of the nature of the app and services. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, applicable legal notification duties will be followed.

The app intentionally targets children aged 6–12, teens aged 13–17 and adults aged 18 and over with equal priority; children aged 5 and under are not part of the target audience.
